Privacy Policy

KGrid — Personal Finance Journal

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Overview

KGrid is built around a single principle: your financial data belongs to you, and it stays on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what data KGrid collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By design, KGrid collects almost nothing. The app is a local journal, tracker, and analyzer — not a service that syncs, shares, or transmits your information.

Data stored on your device

KGrid stores the following information locally, inside the app sandbox on your iPhone:

  • Accounts (name, type, balance, currency)
  • Transactions (date, amount, category, account, notes)
  • Investments (symbol, quantity, cost basis, asset class)
  • Budgets (category, monthly limit, period)
  • Goals (name, target amount, target date, current progress)
  • Receipts (photos you capture or import, attached to transactions)

This data is stored in a local file in the app's sandbox. It is never transmitted to a server, never shared with a third party, and never used for advertising or analytics.

Data we do NOT collect

KGrid does not collect, access, or transmit:

  • Bank account credentials or login information
  • Brokerage account credentials or login information
  • Credit card numbers or payment instrument details
  • Real-time market quotes or any financial market data feeds
  • Personal identifiers, contact lists, or device identifiers for tracking
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports tied to you, or advertising identifiers

Because KGrid does not collect this information, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose.

Photos and receipts

If you choose to attach a receipt to a transaction, the photo is stored locally on your device, inside the app sandbox. KGrid does not upload receipt photos to any server.

To capture a receipt, KGrid will request access to your camera. To import an existing photo, KGrid will request access to your photo library. These permissions are used solely to let you add a receipt to a transaction and are processed entirely on-device.

Backups

Your data is included in standard iOS device backups (iCloud or iTunes) only to the extent you have enabled those backups at the system level. KGrid does not perform its own cloud sync and does not operate any backup service.

If you delete KGrid, the app's local sandbox — including all accounts, transactions, holdings, budgets, goals, and receipts — is removed. KGrid has no separate copy of your data to delete.

Children's privacy

KGrid is intended for users aged 16 and older. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because KGrid does not collect personal information from anyone, this policy applies uniformly.

Changes to this policy

If KGrid's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent change. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or an app update.

Your rights

Because KGrid does not collect or transmit your financial data, there is no data held by us to access, export, or delete. Your data lives on your device, where you can manage it through standard iOS controls (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → KGrid).

To export or back up your data, you may use the iOS share or backup features on your device.
