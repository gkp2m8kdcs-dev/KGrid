KGrid

Your private, on-device personal finance journal. Net worth, investments, budgets, and goals — all in one place, all stored locally.

See the full picture of your money

KGrid is a finance journal for people who want clarity without giving a third party their bank credentials. Every account, transaction, holding, budget, goal, and receipt lives in a local file inside the app sandbox. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is synced, nothing leaves your iPhone.

What you can do

Net Worth Dashboard

A single card that shows total assets, investments, and debt, followed by month-to-date income, expense, savings, and savings rate — each with a twelve-month trend sparkline.

Portfolio Journal

Track stocks, ETFs, bonds, mutual funds, REITs, and crypto with manual cost basis, average cost, unrealized P&L, and per-holding return. KGrid computes returns from the numbers you enter.

Budgets

Group categories, set monthly limits, and watch a real-time progress ring warn you before you overspend.

Savings Goals

Turn targets like an emergency fund or a down payment into a live progress story with days remaining and the monthly contribution required.

Receipts

Capture or import photos of invoices and attach them to the corresponding transaction for a clean audit trail.

Reports

A multi-range income-versus-expense bar chart, a daily net-flow line chart, a category donut, and a 12-week spending heatmap you can scrub to spot patterns.

Privacy first. Always.

KGrid is designed as a journal, tracker, and analyzer — never a trading platform, never a brokerage, never a source of investment advice. It never fetches live market quotes and never asks for a brokerage login. Your financial data stays on your device, where it belongs.
