Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All of your accounts, transactions, investments, budgets, goals, and receipts are stored locally on your iPhone, inside the app sandbox. KGrid does not upload your data to any server.

Do I need to connect a bank or brokerage account?

No. KGrid is a manual journal. You enter your own numbers. KGrid never asks for bank or brokerage credentials and never connects to a financial institution.

Does KGrid fetch live stock prices?

No. KGrid computes returns, gains, and portfolio analytics from the cost basis and quantity you enter. This means it works without a signal, anywhere, any time.

How do I back up my data?

KGrid does not perform its own cloud backup. Your data is included in standard iOS device backups if you have iCloud or iTunes backups enabled. To make a local copy, you can use the iOS Files app to access the KGrid sandbox through the Files provider.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Deleting KGrid removes the local sandbox, which includes all of your stored data. Because KGrid has no separate copy of your data, it is permanently deleted with the app. We recommend exporting or backing up before uninstalling.

Does KGrid provide investment advice?

No. KGrid is a journal, tracker, and analyzer. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. The numbers and charts it shows are computed from the data you enter.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

KGrid is designed for iPhone on iOS 16 and above.

Can I import data from another app?

KGrid is designed as a fresh start. You can begin by adding your accounts and entering starting balances, then log transactions, holdings, budgets, and goals as you go.

Reporting a bug

When reporting a bug, please include:

  1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  2. The version of KGrid (Settings → KGrid in the app)
  3. A clear description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it
  4. Screenshots or screen recordings, if possible
